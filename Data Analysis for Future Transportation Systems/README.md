## 1. Data Analysis of a Racecar 

- **Course:** ***Data Analysis for Future Transportation System***
    - **Instructors:** Prof. P. Chiariotti
- **Authors:** M. Cigada, F. D'Agostini, L. Gazzarrini
- **Date:** September 2024 - December 2024
- **Keywords:** Space/Time Domain Analysis, Frequency Domain Analysis, Filtering, Power Spectral Density, Coherence, Time Frequency
- **Software used:** MATLAB
- **Goal of the project:** Clean and structure raw telemetry data to compare the performance of a gentleman driver versus a pro driver. Analyze suspension damper data in both the time and frequency domains—using Power Spectral Density (PSD) and coherence—to identify the first natural frequency of the car's vertical dynamics. Summarize the dynamic behavior using time-frequency spectrograms
